🎓 From the Heart of Kakamega to the World: A Testament to the Power of Sponsorship

My name is Cetrick Kamina. I come from Shinyalu Constituency, Kakamega County, Kenya. Today, I stand proud as a Registered BScN Nurse.

There was a time when my dream felt impossible due to school fees. The gap between my potential and ability to pay was wide.

Then, EducationCHV stepped in. They didn't just offer financial assistance; they offered me a lifeline. Through sponsorship, I navigated secondary and transitioned to university. They believed in me.

University was challenging, but with supporters behind me, I worked harder. I poured my heart into my studies.

Today, I am a fully registered BScN graduate. Every day, I put on my scrubs and serve—often back in rural Kakamega—holding a radio to coordinate community health or attending patients.

My story is proof talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. Sponsoring a child unlocks a future nurse for Kakamega.

— Cetrick Kamina, Registered BScN Nurse | Uzima University | Shinyalu, Kakamega | RIGHT light blue | KSh 700k = One Nurse

Cleophas Munika, Chuka University 2021 - KSh 155,587

🎓 From Shinyalu to Chuka to the Hospitality World

My name is Cleophas Munika from Shinyalu, Kakamega. Today, I am a Chuka University Tourism Management graduate 2017-2021.

In final year 2020-2021, I needed KSh 155,587 to graduate: Tuition 33,200 + Rent 42,000 (2 sem + 4,500x4 Nairobi attachment) + Accommodation/Food 36,800 + Research/License/Report 43,587.

There were moments I thought I would not finish. Then EducationCHV stepped in—a lifeline. They covered final year and made university possible. Their belief motivated me.

From Chuka classes to Nairobi attachment was challenging, but supporters pushed me to excel in Tourism Management.

Today, I am a graduate working in hospitality, wearing uniform with pride, managing tourism services using Chuka knowledge, committed to impact community.

My encounter with CHV Education Team was meaningful and transformative. Their support changed my life forever. I recognize them as important part of my journey.

KSh 155,587 can create a university graduate. Sponsoring unlocks a hospitality leader for Kenya.

— Cleophas Munika, Chuka 2017-2021 Tourism | KSh 155,587 = 33,200+42,000+36,800+43,587 = One Graduate
