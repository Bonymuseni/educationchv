Who We Are

Vision: To have a future empowered with ICT skills for self-reliance and growth of society.

Mission: To offer empowerment training to utilize untapped talents for better livelihoods.

We equip vulnerable youth (KCPE/KCSE leavers, grade D+) with IT literacy, business skills and servant leadership to reduce idleness, drug abuse, early marriages and unemployment in Shinyalu.
