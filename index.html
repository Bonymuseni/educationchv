🎓 From the Heart of Kakamega to the World: A Testament to the Power of Sponsorship

My name is Cetrick Kamina from Shinyalu, Kakamega. Today I am a Registered BScN Nurse.

There was a time when my dream felt impossible due to school fees. The gap between my potential and ability to pay was wide and threatened my future.

Then EducationCHV stepped in. They didn't just offer money; they offered a lifeline. Through sponsorship I navigated secondary and transitioned to university. They believed in me when I needed it most.

University was challenging, but knowing I had supporters behind me pushed me to work harder. I poured my heart into studies, determined to make the most of this opportunity.

Today I am a fully registered BScN graduate. Every day I put on scrubs and serve—often back in rural Kakamega—holding a radio to coordinate community health or attending patients with purpose.

My story proves talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. When you sponsor a child, you unlock a future nurse for Kakamega.

To EducationCHV team and sponsors: Thank you. Your investment transformed my life. I promise to pay it forward serving patients daily.

To young people facing odds: do not give up. Keep working hard. Breakthrough is possible.

To anyone considering sponsorship: please do it. Your kindness turns a struggling student into a lifesaving professional.

— Cetrick Kamina, BScN Nurse Uzima | RIGHT light blue | KSh 700k = One Nurse for Kakamega County

Cleophas Munika, Chuka University 2017-2021 - KSh 155,587 Final Year

🎓 From Shinyalu to Chuka to the Hospitality World: A Journey of Hope

My name is Cleophas Munika from Shinyalu, Kakamega. Today Chuka University Tourism Management graduate 2017-2021.

In final year 2020-2021 I needed KSh 155,587 to graduate. Breakdown: Tuition KSh 33,200, Rent KSh 42,000 (2 sem + 4,500x4 Nairobi attachment), Accommodation and Food KSh 36,800, Research Project License and Report Writing KSh 43,587. This amount stood between me and degree.

There were moments I thought I would not finish. Then EducationCHV stepped in—a lifeline. Through commitment they covered final year and made university possible. Their belief motivated me to work hard and give back.

University challenging—from Chuka classes to Nairobi attachment—but supporters pushed me to excel in Tourism Management.

Today I am a graduate working in hospitality. Every day I wear uniform with pride, serving guests, managing tourism services using Chuka knowledge. I am committed to impact community.

My encounter with CHV Education Team was meaningful and transformative. Their support changed my life forever. I proudly recognize them as important part of my journey.

My story proves KSh 155,587 can create a university graduate. Sponsoring unlocks a hospitality leader for Kenya.

To EducationCHV, Boniface Museni and sponsors: Asante sana. Thank you for believing in my potential. Your support made lasting difference.

To young person in Shinyalu facing fees: do not give up. Keep working hard. Graduation is possible.

To anyone considering sponsorship: please do it. Your KSh 155,000 can turn struggling student into university graduate.

— Cleophas Munika, Chuka 2017-2021 Tourism | 33,200+42,000+36,800+43,587 = 155,587 = One Graduate
